The Doughnut potential: setting the pore of a protein ring

Four films of a protein ring forming out of noise. RFdiffusion3 builds the backbone over 200 steps; until step 158 the Doughnut potential pushes every atom towards a circle of radius r0 on the symmetry axis, so whatever is inside gets pushed out. Then the push stops and RFdiffusion3 finishes the ring on its own. The four panels differ only in how hard it pushes: weight 20, 30, 40, 50. The top row looks down that axis, the middle row from the side, the trace below shows when the push is on, and the dashed circle is r0. Each panel shows the most typical backbone that passed our shape filter, and how many passed. Tap a film and turn the phone sideways.
